M.M.F Publisher is a creator-focused web service for connecting an authorized TikTok account, preparing owned or licensed media, reviewing publishing settings, and submitting user-approved posts through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Features

TikTok account connectionSecure authorization through TikTok Login Kit with explicit user consent.
Media preparationCreators select their own video content and prepare captions and publishing settings.
Publishing controlsPosts are submitted only after the creator reviews the content and initiates the publishing action.
Publishing statusThe service can display the status of user-initiated publishing requests and relevant errors.

How it works

The creator opens M.M.F Publisher and chooses Connect TikTok.
TikTok displays its official authorization and consent screen.
The creator selects media they own or are authorized to publish.
The creator reviews the post settings and explicitly confirms publishing.
M.M.F Publisher submits the request through TikTok's official Content Posting API and shows the resulting status.

Security & user control

OAuth credentials and access tokens are handled server-side and are never intentionally embedded in public client code. The service requests only the TikTok permissions needed for the enabled workflow. Users can revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings.

Support & policies

Questions about account connection, publishing, privacy, or service behavior can be raised through the official M.M.F project support channel. Policy links are always visible below.
