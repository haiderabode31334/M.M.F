Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

M.M.F Publisher is a personal content-management tool that connects to TikTok through official TikTok APIs. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is used.

Information we process

When an authorized user connects a TikTok account, the app may process information made available through the permissions granted by that user, such as the TikTok account identifier, basic profile information, authorization tokens, and publishing-related status information. The app may also process media files, captions, and metadata selected by the user for publishing.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized user, prepare user-selected content, submit publishing requests to TikTok, display publishing status, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical issues.

User control and publishing

M.M.F Publisher does not publish content without an explicit user action. The user chooses the media and initiates each publishing request. The user remains responsible for ensuring that uploaded content is owned by them or that they have permission to publish it.

Sharing of information

Information is not sold. Data is shared with TikTok only as necessary to provide the requested TikTok integration and may be processed by infrastructure providers only when required to operate the service.

Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not exposed in public client code. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access.

Retention and deletion

Authorization information is retained only while needed to provide the integration or until access is revoked. Temporary publishing data may be removed after processing or retained for a limited period for status and troubleshooting purposes. An authorized user may revoke TikTok access through TikTok account settings.

Children

The service is not intended for children and is intended only for users who are legally permitted to use TikTok and this integration.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal requirements change. The current version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the official M.M.F project profile or repository associated with this application.
