Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

M.M.F Publisher is a creator-focused content publishing service that connects authorized TikTok accounts through official TikTok APIs. This policy explains the information the service processes and how it is used.

Information we process

When a user connects a TikTok account, the service may process information made available through the permissions granted by that user, including a TikTok account identifier, basic profile information, authorization tokens, and publishing-related status information. The service may also process media files, captions, and metadata selected by the user for publishing.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized users, provide account connection features, prepare user-selected content, submit user-approved publishing requests to TikTok, display publishing status, maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

User control

M.M.F Publisher is designed so that a user chooses the media, reviews relevant publishing settings, and explicitly initiates publishing. Users remain responsible for ensuring that their content is owned by them or that they have permission to publish it.

Sharing of information

Personal information is not sold. Data is shared with TikTok only when necessary to provide the TikTok integration. Infrastructure providers may process limited information when required to operate, secure, or maintain the service.

Security

OAuth client secrets and access tokens are handled server-side and are not intentionally exposed in public client code. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Retention and deletion

Authorization information is retained only while needed to provide the connected service or until access is revoked, expires, or is otherwise no longer required. Temporary publishing data may be removed after processing or retained for a limited period for status, security, and troubleshooting purposes. Users may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings.

Children

The service is not directed to children and may only be used by people legally permitted to use TikTok and this integration.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, platform requirements, or applicable rules change. The current version will remain available on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the official M.M.F project support channel associated with this service.
